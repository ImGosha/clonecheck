# Sentences to read aloud

Read all 30 in order in one recording, in your normal voice.
Pause for about two seconds after every sentence.

## English

1. Hi Grandma, it's me. Can you hear me okay?
2. I'm in a bit of trouble and I need your help right now.
3. Please don't tell Mom and Dad, I don't want them to worry.
4. I had a car accident and I need money to fix it today.
5. Can you send two thousand dollars? I will pay you back next week.
6. Practice starts at six tomorrow morning, so we have to leave early.
7. I finished my homework and now I'm going to the rink.
8. The weather in Milwaukee was cold and windy all weekend.
9. We won the game on Saturday, three goals to one.
10. Could you pick me up after school at half past three?
11. I left my phone charger at your house last night.
12. The bus was late again, so I walked the whole way home.
13. Let's have dinner together on Sunday, I'll bring dessert.
14. My new phone number is different, please save this one.
15. Thank you so much, I love you, talk to you soon.

## Russian
